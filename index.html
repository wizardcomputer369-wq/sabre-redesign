About Sabre Lighting

Sabre Lighting is an Irish‑owned company specialising in street and sports lighting solutions. We work with councils, developers and clubs to deliver safe, reliable and efficient lighting systems.

With years of experience in design, specification and project delivery, we combine engineering expertise with a practical, hands‑on approach.

Our focus is on quality, compliance with standards, and long‑term performance for every installation.
